Custom software

Custom software costs less than you think.

AI-assisted development has dramatically cut the time it takes to build software. If you're paying significant fees for third-party tools that only half fit your business, owning your own solution may now cost less over time, and work the way you actually do.

Get a cost check → How it works
The problem

Renting software adds up.

Off-the-shelf software made sense when building your own was slow and expensive. That math has changed.

Fees that grow with you

Per-seat and per-module pricing means your software bill climbs every time you hire, and again at every renewal.

Paying for what you don't use

Most teams use a fraction of what they're paying for, while the one feature they need is missing or locked behind a higher tier.

Bending your business to fit

Spreadsheets and workarounds fill the gaps, your data lives in someone else's system, and your process follows the tool instead of the other way around.

How I help

  1. Cost check

    We compare what you spend on third-party software today with what a custom replacement would take, so the decision is about real numbers.

  2. Build the essentials

  3. Own it and grow it

Signs it's time
